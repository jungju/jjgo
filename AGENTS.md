## Editorial harness

Before adding or changing any public text in `app/`, read `docs/editorial-harness.md` and apply its site-wide review. This includes Korean and English copy, metadata, image alt text, buttons, project descriptions, and Notes. For a Notes article, also complete the article brief and voice review in that document. The existing personal essay in `app/notes/eternal-sunshine-content.ts` is a provisional in-repository voice reference; do not infer that every sentence in the repository was written by the site owner.

Keep claims tied to supplied or verifiable facts. Do not invent autobiographical events, results, metrics, or quotes to make text sound personal. Keep hypothetical examples recognizable as such. Automated checks verify structure and pages, not personal authenticity. The owner has authorized `jjgo-blog-writing` executions to generate a cover, prepare bilingual content, verify, and deploy without another approval round. Honor explicit draft-only requests.

For a new Notes article based on a movie or a GeekNews item, use the personal `jjgo-blog-writing` skill in addition to the editorial harness. The site owner chose the Eternal Sunshine article as the style reference for this workflow.
